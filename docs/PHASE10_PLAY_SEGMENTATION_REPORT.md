# Phase 10 Play Segmentation Report

Generated by `benchmarks/render_phase10_report.py` from `outputs/phase10_segmentation_benchmark.json` (benchmark `phase10_v1`, package `0.5.0`, kind `synthetic_segmentation_benchmark`). Do not edit by hand.

## What was built

`football_vision/analytics/segmentation.py` implements ROADMAP Phase 10 (PLAY START -> PRE-SNAP -> SNAP -> PLAY -> PLAY END) on top of the untouched Phase 1-4 contracts:

* `PlayTimestampLabel` (schema) — manual timestamps, the primary contract. A play `start_frame` is always manual; `snap_frame` / `end_frame` may be omitted.
* `build_motion_evidence` / `MotionEvidence` — per-frame collective-motion evidence from Phase 4 `FieldTrajectory` samples, with complete sample accounting: every sample is either usable measured evidence or counted as excluded dead-reckoning / rejected / coasted / unprojected.
* `CollectiveMotionSegmenter` — snap as the first sustained collective-motion onset after a fully defined quiescent baseline; play end as the first sustained collective quiet run after sustained activity. Both refuse with a named reason.
* `PlaySegmenter` — the entry point. Combines manual boundaries with automatic estimates, validates non-overlap, aggregates coordinate provenance, and emits `PlaySegment` / `PlaySegmentationResult` with per-frame phase labels and audit counters.
* `PlaySegment.phase_for` — per-frame phase machine `outside_play / pre_snap / snap / play / play_end / snap_unknown`. A refused snap yields `snap_unknown`, never a guessed split.
* `PlaySegment.to_play_record()` — projection onto the Phase 16A `PlayRecord` contract (metrics arrive with Phases 11-13); an unresolved play end exports as the explicit sentinel `-1` plus a note.

## How to read these numbers

* **snap_error_frames** — |detected snap frame - fixture snap frame|; fixture snap = first frame a mover crosses the speed threshold
* **snap_exact_match_rate** — exact-frame snap matches / snaps resolved (denominator excludes refusals; refusals are counted separately)
* **end_error_frames** — |detected play-end frame - fixture dead-ball frame|; detected end = first frame of the sustained quiet run
* **phase_frame_accuracy** — frames whose predicted phase equals the fixture phase / all fixture-labelled frames (refusals score as mismatches, they are not dropped)
* **false_refusal** — the fixture declares a snap and the segmenter refused to emit one
* **true_refusal** — the fixture declares no determinable snap and the segmenter refused
* **expectation_violation** — a frozen manifest expectation (error bound or required refusal reason) was not met
* **confidence** — a priori contrast x usable-evidence coverage, capped at 0.99; NOT a statistically calibrated probability

This benchmark is `synthetic_segmentation_benchmark`: player motion is synthetic and deterministic, ground-truth boundaries are properties of the fixture construction, and no image, detector, homography or whistle signal is involved. `image_detector_quantitative_metrics` is `None` and `end_to_end_video_segmentation_measured` is `False`. Nothing here is evidence about real broadcast video.

## Refusal audit

6 of 11 plays were refused. 4 are *false* refusals — the fixture declares a snap the segmenter did not emit — and they are the documented price of a refusal-first design on low-visibility strata:

* `insufficient_tracks` (`seg_seq_03`): a 4-track close-up cannot support a moving fraction; guessing would be fabrication.
* `no_sustained_onset` (`seg_seq_05`): only 35% of the visible group ever produces reliable measured motion (pile / heavy occlusion), below the 0.45 onset fraction.
* `coordinate_frame_change` (`seg_seq_06`): a mid-play camera cut changes the coordinate frame; speeds are not comparable across it, so the window is refused instead of being segmented anyway.
* `baseline_evidence_gaps` (`seg_seq_07`): frames whose usable measured tracks fall below `min_tracks` are *unknown* motion, not quiescence, so no pre-snap baseline can be established.

2 refusals are *true*: the fixture declares no determinable snap (`no_quiescent_baseline` on an already-moving warm-up window, `insufficient_measured_samples` on a dead-reckoning-only window), and the segmenter labelled those frames `snap_unknown` rather than splitting them.

Every refusal carries its reason in `SnapEstimate.reason` and in `PlaySegmentationResult.refusals`; `fabricated_snap_frames` and `fabricated_play_ends` are 0, and `expectation_violations` is empty, meaning every frozen manifest pass criterion (error bound or required refusal reason) was met.

## Provenance and semantics (from the manifest)

* **player_motion** — Synthetic deterministic field-space speed profiles (rectangular onset/cessation, staggered ramps, partial-group motion, pre-snap movers). Timings and speeds are engineering fixtures, NOT recorded NFL player trajectories.
* **trajectories** — FieldTrajectory / TrajectorySample objects constructed directly in field space. They are NOT the output of the Phase 1-4 image pipeline on video: no frames are decoded, no homography is fitted, no detector runs. Phase 10 is measured as a consumer of Phase 4 contracts.
* **ground_truth** — Snap and play-end frames are properties of the fixture construction (the frame the first mover crosses the speed threshold, the frame motion stops). There is no whistle audio, no referee signal, no independently annotated broadcast clip and no real play clock.
* **geometry** — Each sequence carries one synthetic coordinate_frame_id ('synthetic:<sequence_id>') except the explicit camera-cut sequence, which switches to a second synthetic frame. No absolute yard line is claimed anywhere; x_coord_mode is 'relative_10yd' by construction.
* **image_detector_accuracy** — UNMEASURED. Phase 10 never sees an image. Nothing in this benchmark is evidence that image-based player detection, tracking or projection is solved.
* **end_to_end_video_accuracy** — UNMEASURED. The unattended video runner (football_vision/evaluation/video.py) does not segment plays, because a play START is always a manual timestamp in this design; no video-to-play-segmentation accuracy exists.
* **team_labels** — Not used. Segmentation is team-agnostic; collective motion does not require offense/defense identity.
* **thresholds** — All thresholds are the frozen a priori defaults in football_vision/analytics/segmentation.py (motion speed 1.0 yd/s, onset fraction 0.45, baseline fraction 0.15, baseline 10 frames, sustained 3 frames, min tracks 6, quiet 6 frames, min play 6 frames). They were not fitted on any split, and TEST was not used to select them.
* **expectations** — Each play declares a frozen 'expected' block (maximum tolerated snap/end error in frames, or the refusal reason that must be produced). Expectations are pass criteria declared before running; 'expectation_violations' counts mismatches and must be 0. An expectation is not a claim that the behaviour is good for real football.
* **refusal_semantics** — A refusal is 'false' when the fixture ground truth contains a snap (or play end) that the segmenter did not emit, and 'true' when the fixture declares no determinable boundary. False refusals are reported, never hidden: they are the documented cost of a refusal-first design on low-visibility strata.

## Boundary conditions (what this phase deliberately does not do)

* No automatic play *start*: the existence of a play is always a manual declaration. An unattended runner cannot segment plays, which is why `football_vision/evaluation/video.py` does not call Phase 10.
* No whistle or dead-ball modelling: the automatic play end is the first frame of a sustained collective quiet run, and it stays opt-in (`allow_automatic_play_end`, default False).
* No re-identification across camera cuts: a coordinate-frame change refuses the whole window instead of stitching incompatible axes.
* No calibrated probabilities: `confidence` is an a priori contrast x coverage score capped at 0.99, reported next to the evidence that produced it.
* No ground truth in production: nothing in the module reads or forwards a label of the true snap; only the benchmark harness compares against fixtures.
